Video: Inspecting sensor data in FlexMeasures

Nicolas Höning
July 23, 2025
Updated on October 8, 2026

You cannot do excellent energy optimization without understanding the data you work with. 

We have developed a lot of features recently, so that you can inspect your data ― with basic stats, the Playback feature, bar charts & heat maps and even manual data upload!

In this 3-minute video, we walk you through all of it.

If you have questions about sensor data capabilities in FlexMeasures, make sure to contact us.
