OpenADR Integration

Nicolas Höning
September 21, 2026

Local congestion support is one of the next big things in European grids, and the OpenADR protocol seems to be in favour by many DSOs. (it's already widely-used in the U.S.) In The Netherlands, 3 DSOs are piloting it with charging hubs.

In The Netherlands, ELaad (innovation lab of Dutch DSOs) is using FlexMeasures and contributed a new plugin: flexmeasures-openadr3 🎉

Thanks to ElaadNL for the code contribution. And I'm excited we are now connecting LFEnergy projects to form full-stack open solutions! (this connects FlexMeasures and OpenLeadr - the OpenADR project focusing more on the DSO side).

If you want to see a full demo of integrating a top-level node (the DSO side) with an end-level node (the EMS side), using the two projects and the new plugin, you are in luck, as we showcased that on the LF Energy Summit Europe 2026:

With ElaadNL's Nick Van Der Burgt, I presented how you can not only use an open protocol between DSO and EMS/aggregator, but also use opensource technology for your local site optimization - which plays nice with the DSO's dynamic requests.

Nick's demo covers entering and broadcasting dynamic congestion signals for the upcoming day. Plus, on the aggregator/EMS side, he shows how to subscribe to these signals and how a new one will influence the BTM planning of FlexMeasures.
