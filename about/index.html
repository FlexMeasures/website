About 

FlexMeasures is an open source project that was originally created by the team at Seita.nl and is now a part of LF Energy. The team at Seita continues to maintain and support the project. 

FlexMeasures was created by a small team of engineers who think that energy optimization, scheduling and forecasting are an essential part of any renewable energy strategy. The energy optimization engines that form the backbone of these systems should be auditable, transparent, extensible and adhere to industry-accepted standards. That’s why the project was not only open sourced for full transparency, but was also donated to a neutral foundation. 

At a time when many companies are looking to LLMs to solve everything, FlexMeasures was designed using mathematical models. It also isn’t designed to be a general energy management system, but rather software specialized in solving scheduling and forecasting, which can either be used as a specialized EMS or as a component of a more general EMS. 

Not an expert in mathematical models, in energy scheduling and forecasting, or in general an expert in implementing energy optimization projects? The team behind FlexMeasures can help make your energy scheduling project a success, from helping make onboarding easier  to helping you translate your specific business logic into a custom energy scheduling and forecasting set-up. 

FlexMeasures is a powerful, enterprise-ready solution for organizations with complex energy scheduling needs. If your customers have started asking for energy scheduling capabilities, you should try it out. 

Our mission is to make energy optimization a part of all energy management systems, reducing overall costs for end users and facilitating the transition to green energy.
