Get Control Over When Your Assets Consume Energy

FlexMeasures is an enterprise-ready & customizable energy optimization engine, for scheduling and forecasting in real-time operations.

 

✅ Integrate energy optimization and scheduling into your energy management system. FlexMeasures can be used for any type of energy usage (heating, EVs, factories and more), any type of energy source (solar, grid, generator). It allows your customers to optimize their energy consumption based on the variables that matter to them, whether it is when the solar panels are producing, fluctuating costs of energy or requirements from the grid operator. 

✅ Audit and understand the optimizations FlexMeasures creates. FlexMeasures is open source and well-documented. It’s fully controllable via a dev/agent-friendly API. In its core, it uses mathematical optimization and no LLMs. The UI allows to change many crucial settings and gives you a playback option to review critical days. Plus, FlexMeasures keeps audit logs on who did what.

✅ Want to get the most out of FlexMeasures, better understand how energy scheduling works or just proactively avoid pitfalls? You can work with the team at Seita, the energy optimization experts who built and maintain FlexMeasures.

FlexMeasures is a project at the Linux Energy Foundation, written in Python. Read more about FlexMeasures on the LF Energy blog.
