Get in touch

SeitaBV
May 30, 2022

We want you to succeed in using, hosting or extending FlexMeasures. For all your questions and ideas, you can join the FlexMeasures community in the following ways: 

  • Join the #flexmeasures channel over at https://slack.lfenergy.org
  • Come by one of the Technical Steering Committee meetings, held via Zoom every six weeks. They're open for beginners (learn what it is) as well as existing users (what goes on the roadmap?). The agenda is here.
  • Write to us at flexmeasures@lists.lfenergy.org (you can join this mailing list here).
  • Follow @flexmeasures on Mastodon

You're also welcome to contribute with issues or pull requests on Github.
