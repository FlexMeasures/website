LF Energy Summit 2024: FlexMeasures Demo

SeitaBV
September 8, 2024

The LF Energy Summit 2024 was a great event, with lots of good ideas, demos and socializing between energy & open source enthusiasts. Yes, the socializing happened with Belgian beer :)

We also gave a 45-minute demo about FlexMeasures. I believe we have seldom gotten so into depth in a public session. Usually, like in Steering Committee meetings or other public settings, we only take a few minutes to outline what FlexMeasures is and what features it has. And we got a lot of interesting questions back, thank you everyone for the great discussion, which lasted another 20 minutes or so.
